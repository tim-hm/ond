What helps us help you

If something is broken, these turn a guess into a fix:

  • Your device and iOS version — Settings › General › About
  • What you did, what happened, and what you expected instead
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if it is something you can see

Common things

Refunds and cancellations
Subscriptions are billed by Apple, not by us, so cancellations and refunds go through them — open Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your device, or ask Apple directly at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue a refund on Apple's behalf, but tell us what went wrong anyway, because it is usually something we should fix.
A subscription you paid for is not showing
Try Restore Purchases on the paywall first — that re-asks the App Store what you own. If it still will not unlock, write to us and say which device and whether you chose the monthly or yearly plan.
Deleting your data
Delete everything yourself at Settings → Delete account → Delete everything. If you use önd anonymously, your history cannot be recovered after the device and its identifier are lost; if you use Sign in with Apple, signing in on another device can recover it before deletion. If you cannot use the in-app control, email us with the Support ID shown under Account in Settings. The details are in the privacy policy.
The haptics feel wrong or absent
Check that Settings › Sounds & Haptics › System Haptics is on, and that Silent Mode is not suppressing them. If the rhythm is right but the strength is not, the app's own Settings screen has a haptic strength dial.
Health questions
We cannot give medical advice, and önd is a wellness app rather than a medical one. If you are pregnant, have epilepsy, a heart or respiratory condition, or a history of fainting, talk to a doctor before starting breathwork — particularly the energising exercises, which the app marks with their own cautions.
